Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This page explains how you can delete your ATLAS account and request deletion of account-related data.

1. Delete your account from ATLAS

If you can access your ATLAS account, you can request account deletion directly from the application.

ATLAS → Profile → Settings → Delete account

Follow the confirmation steps shown by ATLAS to complete the deletion process.

2. Request deletion without access to the application

If you cannot access ATLAS, you may request account deletion by contacting ChrisDevLabs from the email address associated with your ATLAS account.

Send the request to: dev.atlastraining@gmail.com

Use a subject such as “ATLAS account deletion request”. Additional information may be requested when necessary to reasonably verify that the request relates to the account in question.

3. Data associated with your ATLAS account

Deleting your ATLAS account removes the account and associated profile information maintained by ATLAS online services, subject to data that must be retained when required for legitimate security, legal or operational purposes.

ATLAS training information is primarily stored locally on your device. Deleting your online account does not necessarily erase information that remains stored locally on that device.

Local information may be removed by deleting it within ATLAS, clearing the application's local storage or uninstalling the application, depending on the information concerned.

4. Google Drive backups and external services

If you previously created an optional ATLAS backup in Google Drive, that backup is associated with the Google account and permissions you authorized.

Deleting your ATLAS account does not necessarily delete data that is independently stored or controlled through a third-party service. You may need to manage that information through the corresponding provider.

5. After deletion

Once the ATLAS account has been deleted, it can no longer be used to sign in to the deleted account. If you later choose to use ATLAS again, you may need to create a new account.

6. Contact

For questions regarding account or data deletion, contact:

dev.atlastraining@gmail.com
