Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

ATLAS is a training application developed by ChrisDevLabs. This Privacy Policy explains what information ATLAS uses, where it may be stored, the services that may process it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information stored on your device

ATLAS stores most training information locally on your device. This may include profile information, date of birth, height, body weight, training experience, preferences or limitations, gyms and available equipment, routines, schedules, workout history, progression information, active workout state, application settings, tutorial progress and your profile image.

Local training information is not automatically uploaded to ChrisDevLabs merely because you use ATLAS.

2. Health Connect

On supported Android devices, ATLAS may request permission to read your body weight from Health Connect. ATLAS does not request permission to write weight data to Health Connect.

The most recent authorized body-weight value may be used when starting a workout. If that value becomes part of an ATLAS workout or session record, it may also be included in backups or cloud synchronization that you choose to enable.

You can revoke ATLAS access to Health Connect at any time through Android Health Connect settings.

3. Google Drive backups

Google Drive backup is optional. If you connect your Google account and grant the required Drive permission, ATLAS may store a private backup in Google's application data area.

A backup may include application settings, onboarding and profile information, gyms, routines and schedules, workout history, progression and deload information, an active workout session and your profile image.

ATLAS requests access only to its own private application data area in Google Drive for this functionality.

4. ATLAS accounts and Supabase

ATLAS uses Supabase for account-related services such as authentication, persistent sessions and account profile data. Supabase may process information such as your email address, authentication and session information, internal account identifiers, username, display name and account access or entitlement information.

Additional ATLAS cloud functionality may use Supabase in the future. When a feature requires additional cloud data processing, this policy may be updated before or when that functionality is made available.

5. Third-party sign-in providers

ATLAS may offer sign-in through third-party identity providers such as Google or Apple. If you choose one of these methods, the provider supplies ATLAS with the information authorized through its consent flow.

Google Drive authorization is separate from account authentication and is used only when you explicitly connect the backup feature.

6. Notifications

ATLAS may use device notifications for features such as rest timers, workout-related reminders and service-related notices. Account or data-retention notices may also be used when relevant account lifecycle functionality is enabled.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings and, where available, through ATLAS settings.

7. Sharing, advertising and service providers

ATLAS does not sell your personal information and does not use personal training or health information for targeted advertising.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to provide a feature you choose to use, including services such as Supabase, Google authentication, Google Drive or Android Health Connect.

8. Data retention and account deletion

Local ATLAS data generally remains on your device until you remove it, restore different data, clear the application's storage or uninstall ATLAS.

Information stored through online services is retained according to the function that generated it and for the time necessary to provide that service.

You can delete your ATLAS account from within the application. ATLAS also provides a public account and data deletion page for users who cannot access the application.

For instructions, visit Account and Data Deletion .

ATLAS may introduce automatic deletion of accounts that remain inactive for a defined period. Such a policy will not be enabled without first defining the applicable inactivity period and providing advance notice where appropriate.

9. Security

ATLAS is designed to minimize unnecessary permissions and data access. Security protections are reviewed during development, including access controls for online account data.

However, no application, device or network service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices

You may decline optional permissions, avoid connecting optional services, revoke Health Connect access, disconnect supported services, manage or delete your ATLAS account, use available backup tools and control notification preferences.

Some ATLAS features may become unavailable when the permission or service they require is disabled.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ATLAS evolves. Material changes affecting how personal information is processed will be reflected in the current version of this policy as appropriate before or when the corresponding functionality becomes available.
